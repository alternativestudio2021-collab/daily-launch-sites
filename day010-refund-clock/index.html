Japan tax-free shopping · from 1 Nov 2026

Tax Refund Clock

From 1 November 2026 shops in Japan charge the full price, tax included, and refund the tax only after customs confirms at departure that you are taking the goods out. Each receipt gets its own 90-day deadline. Add your receipts below and enter your flight date to see which ones still qualify.

Tax back is the consumption tax in the price (10/110 of the 10% part, 8/108 of the 8% part for food and drinks). It is shown before any refund fee: the method, timing and fee are set by each shop or its refund company, so check the shop's notice.

At the airport or port

  1. Find the tax-free customs terminal before you check in bags

    Customs confirms your purchases at a kiosk or electronic terminal at the airport or seaport. Do it before you hand over checked baggage, so the goods are still with you if customs asks to see them.

  2. Scan your passport

    The terminal reads the purchase records the shops sent under your passport. Use the same passport you showed when you bought.

  3. Have every item of every receipt with you

    Customs confirms one receipt at a time. If even one tax-free item from a receipt is missing, nothing on that receipt is confirmed. Food, drinks and cosmetics you used or ate in Japan cannot be refunded.

  4. If customs asks, show the goods

    You may be directed to a customs counter to show the items. Keep them easy to reach.

  5. Get the refund the way the shop said

    After customs confirms, the shop or its refund company pays you back, for example to a card or account you registered. Timing and fees depend on the shop.

What changed on 1 November 2026

Payment

You pay the tax first

No more tax-free price at the till. You pay the tax-inclusive price and the tax comes back after departure.

Deadline

90 days per purchase

If you leave after the deadline for a receipt, that purchase is treated as an ordinary taxed sale and no refund is paid.

Limits

One minimum, no cap on consumables

General goods and consumables are now added together toward the ¥5,000 (before tax) minimum at one shop on one day. The ¥500,000 cap on consumables and the sealed bag are gone.

Before 1 Nov

Old receipts, old rules

Purchases made up to 31 October 2026 were sold under the old system, with the tax removed at the till. Follow the instructions you got for those.

Questions

I bought things in Osaka on 2 Nov and fly home on 5 Feb. Do I get the tax back?

No. The deadline for a 2 Nov purchase is 31 Jan. Leaving on 5 Feb is too late for that receipt. Receipts dated 7 Nov or later would still be within 90 days.

I ate one box of snacks from a receipt with five boxes.

That receipt as a whole cannot be confirmed, because customs checks each purchase record as a unit. If you shop for things you plan to open in Japan, pay for them on a separate receipt.

Is the refund always the full 10%?

The tax in a tax-inclusive price is 10/110 (about 9.1%) of the price, or 8/108 for food and drinks. Some shops or refund companies deduct a fee; others, such as the PPIH group (Don Quijote and others), announced they will not charge travellers a fee, with a fee for cash pickup at the airport.

Who can shop tax-free?

Visitors who are not residents of Japan, such as people on a short-term stay. The shop checks your passport when you buy.
